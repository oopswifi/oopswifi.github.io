About the game

You only came to fix the Wi-Fi on the set of a dating show. Then the male lead ran off in the night, and the director cast you instead. Oops! I'm Just Here to Fix the Wi-Fi is a first-person romantic comedy (a visual novel) with photo-real pictures: five women, a shot for every line, timed choices, live comments and the guests' group chat, 28 endings (regret endings and a TRUE END in the Special among them) and a hidden route. It plays in a phone or computer browser, with nothing to download. Contains romance scenes (swimwear, kissing); no nudity or sexual content.

It plays in English, Simplified Chinese or Traditional Chinese: switch with 简 / 繁 / EN on the title screen or in Settings. A browser set to a language other than Chinese starts in English.

Screen Reader Mode

A screen-reader mode lets blind and low-vision players play the free demo with NVDA, VoiceOver, TalkBack and other screen readers. Turn it on with the first Tab stop on the title screen, in Settings, or by opening https://oopswifi.github.io/?a11y=1.

  • Every line is read once with its speaker; a new picture is described first, and chat messages say who sent them.
  • At a choice, the focus moves to the first answer: Tab moves between answers and Enter picks one, or press its number. No choice has a countdown.
  • Space or Enter goes on; Esc opens the menu (save, load, history, settings); F5 / F9 quick save and quick load.
  • It turns off timed choices, minigames (counted as wins), live comments, camera sway and screen shake, and shows text at once; each can be turned back on in Settings.
  • Not finished yet: pictures from episode 2 on only say who is in them, and the checkout is Payhip's page, not yet fully tested with a screen reader. If anything gets in your way, email icodewoods@gmail.com and we will help you buy.

Privacy

No account is needed and the game collects no personal data; saves stay in your browser. Payments are processed by Payhip, Stripe and PayPal; we never see your card number or other payment details.

To learn which channels bring players, the game counts anonymous events with Umami (for example how many people start the game, reach the end of episode 1 or tap buy). No cookies, no ID that identifies you, and never your name, saves or code. Nothing is counted when your browser sends Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control. You can also switch on “Opt out of anonymous stats” (不参与匿名统计) in the game's Settings (设置): after that nothing is sent, and playing, sharing and buying work as before.
