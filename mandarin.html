2. A question before you answer

优化 usually means "to optimize, to improve". Is HR improving the programmer's job? And what do the quotation marks around 优化 and 毕业 tell you?

Show the answer

No: the job is being cut. In an HR meeting, 优化 is a euphemism for a layoff, and 毕业 ("graduating") is another. The quotation marks show that the narrator hears the spin, and so does his line about 那个只用来开除人的小会议室, the room used only for firing people.

Three ways to answer:

  • 去！三千一天，我连夜出发。去！三千一天，我連夜出發。
    Qù! Sānqiān yì tiān, wǒ liányè chūfā.
    I'm in! Three thousand a day — I'll leave tonight.
  • 不去，我想先躺平三个月。不去，我想先躺平三個月。
    Bú qù, wǒ xiǎng xiān tǎngpíng sān ge yuè.
    No. I want to "lie flat" for three months first. (→ a 翻车结局 in the game)
  • 先问清楚：有没有坑？先問清楚：有沒有坑？
    Xiān wèn qīngchu: yǒu méiyǒu kēng?
    Let me ask first: is there a catch?

5. Words to keep

优化
優化		yōuhuàto optimize
From HR, a polite word for cutting a job: you are being laid off.
毕业
畢業		bìyèto graduate
Office slang for being let go, as if leaving were an achievement.
被裁
被裁		bèi cáito be laid off
The plain way to say it (裁 cut, as in 裁员 layoffs).
急活儿
急活兒		jí huóra rush job
活儿 is work or a gig; the -r ending is northern and casual.
日结
日結		rìjiépaid daily
Literally "settled each day": cash at the end of every day.
包吃包住
包吃包住		bāo chī bāo zhùmeals and a bed included
包 "cover" + 吃 eat + 住 stay: a common line in job ads.
躺平
躺平		tǎngpíngto lie flat
To opt out of the rat race and do the minimum.
坑
坑		kēnga pit; a catch
有没有坑？ — "is there a catch?"
