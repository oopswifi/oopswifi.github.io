Oops! I'm Just Here to Fix the Wi-Fi (糟了！我只是来恋综修网线的) is an interactive romantic comedy (visual novel) by the independent developer iCodeWoods, in English, Simplified Chinese and Traditional Chinese, played in a phone or desktop web browser with no download or account. A laid-off programmer comes to fix the Wi-Fi on a dating show and is cast as its male contestant: 5 women and a hidden route, timed choices, 28 endings. The prologue and episode 1 are free; the full game is a one-time US$1 activation code (Payhip). The images are AI-generated, and so is the optional Chinese voice acting of the prologue and episode 1; the story and code were written by the developer with an AI assistant. All characters are fictional adults. Contains romance scenes (swimwear, kissing); no nudity or sexual content. Website: https://oopswifi.github.io/
